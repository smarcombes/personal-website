What I'm exploring

Making AI useful to the 99%, across three axes:

Removing constraints — enabling agents to work and evolve without humans: account creation, permissions, API access, machine setup.

Ensuring a safe environment — agents acting on your behalf without ever holding your credentials, with a human in the loop where it counts.

Improving the UX — systems that are frictionless when you know what you want, and insightful when you don't.
