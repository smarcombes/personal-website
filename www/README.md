## What I'm Up To

Exploring ways to make AI useful to _the 99%_, across three axes:

- **Removing constraints** — enabling agents to work and evolve without humans, by eliminating human-bound friction (account creation, permissions, API access, machine and tooling setup, ...)
- **Ensuring a safe environment** — letting agents act on behalf of users without ever holding their credentials, have the tools to put a human in the loop, etc.
- **Improving the UX** — systems that are frictionless when you know what you want, and insightful & self-sufficient when you don't
